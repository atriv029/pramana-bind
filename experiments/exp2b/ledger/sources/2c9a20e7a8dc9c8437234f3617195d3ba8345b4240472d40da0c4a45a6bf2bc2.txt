A natural question is whether it makes sense to use multiple prototypes per class instead of just one.If the number of prototypes per class is fixed and greater than 1, then this would require a partitioning scheme to further cluster the support points within a class. This has been proposed in Mensink et al. (2013) and Rippel et al. (2016); however both methods require a separate partitioning phase that is decoupled from the weight updates, while our approach is simple to learn with ordinary gradient descent methods.
