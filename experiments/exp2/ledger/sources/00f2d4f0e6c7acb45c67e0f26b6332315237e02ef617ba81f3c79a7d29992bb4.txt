In politics , president is a title given to leaders of republican states .   The functions exercised by a president vary according to the form of government . In parliamentary republics , they are limited to those of the head of state , and are thus largely ceremonial . In presidential and semi - presidential republics , the role of the president is more prominent , encompassing also ( in most cases ) the functions of the head of government . In authoritarian regimes , a dictator or leader of a one - party state may also be called a president .
