Each network is trained with identical settings and tested at 256\times 256, single crop accuracy. Run times are measured on a Titan X at 256\times 256. Thus Darknet-53 performs on par with state-of-the-art classifiers but with fewer floating point operations and more speed. Darknet-53 is better than ResNet-101 and 1.5×1.5\times1.5 × faster. Darknet-53 has similar performance to ResNet-152 and is 2×2\times2 × faster.Darknet-53 also achieves the highest measured floating point operations per second. This means the network structure better utilizes the GPU, making it more efficient to evaluate and thus faster. That’s mostly because ResNets have just way too many layers and aren’t very efficient.
