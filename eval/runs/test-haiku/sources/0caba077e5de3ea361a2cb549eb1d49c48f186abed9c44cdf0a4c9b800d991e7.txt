Age Limits:
(a)A candidate for this examination must have attained the age of 21 years and must not have attained the age of 30 years on
the 1 st January, 2020 i.e., he/she must have been born not earlier than 2 nd January, 1990 and not later than 1 st January, 1999.
(b) The upper age‐limit of 30 years will be relaxable upto 35 years in the case of Government servants of the following categories,
if they are employed in a Department/ Office under the control of any of the authorities mentioned in column 1 below and apply
for admission to the examination for all or any of the Service(s)/Post(s) mentioned in column 2, for which they are otherwise
eligible.
