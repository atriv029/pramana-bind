# Demonstration

The current demo is the interactive page `Pramana_Bind_demo.html` (hosted-ready copy: `hosting/index.html`).
Walkthroughs and the recording script are in `hosting/WALKTHROUGH.md`; hosting notes in `hosting/README.md`.
It is built by `interactive/extract_data.py` and `interactive/build.py` from the frozen artifacts and tested offline by `interactive/test_demo.js`.

Earlier certificate viewers (`viewer_exp2.html`, `viewer.html`) and their ledgers are kept for the record.
